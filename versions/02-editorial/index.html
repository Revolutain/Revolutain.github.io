01
What we do

Services, organized the way engagements run

Every relationship starts with a diagnostic. From there a client may commission a build, adopt a recommended set of tools with training for the people who will use them, or do both. The Sustain stage keeps what was built running and keeps us involved as a standing advisor.

Stage 1

Diagnose

Operational Diagnostic

The diagnostic opens every relationship. It is led by a partner, priced as a fixed fee, and takes two to three weeks.

What we do
  • Interview the owner, the operations and finance leads, and two to four frontline staff
  • Inventory the systems in use, where the data lives, how it moves, and what breaks
  • Run a time study of the most repetitive workflows, combining self-reporting with sampled observation
  • Review security and data handling: access, backups, credentials, vendor exposure
What you receive
  • A ranked opportunity map: interventions with estimated hours saved, revenue effect, cost, and risk
  • A recommended set of tools matched to the processes we observed, and a plan for training the team to use them
  • A written report, a 60-minute readout with the owner, and a 90-day plan
  • Fixed-price quotes for the top two or three interventions

The diagnostic fee is credited against any build started within 60 days.

Stage 2

Build and Train

Decision Science Builds

Turn data into decisions. Typical builds: a demand forecast, a pricing and margin analysis, a KPI framework with an executive dashboard, a customer or job profitability model, capacity and scheduling optimization, forecast-driven budgeting. We work on your existing databases, build the models in Python, and deliver dashboards with automated refresh.

Infrastructure and Data Platform Builds

Fix the plumbing. Typical builds: consolidating spreadsheets into a governed database; integrating point-of-sale, CRM, accounting, and scheduling systems; a reporting layer over the systems you already run; migrating away from legacy Access and Excel; backups, access control, and credential hygiene; right-sizing cloud spend.

Workflow Automation and LLM Integration

Remove repetitive work. Typical builds: document intake and extraction (invoices, contracts, intake forms) into structured records; voice-to-record documentation; drafting assistants grounded in your own documents; email and ticket triage; report narration; pipelines that monitor data and escalate exceptions.

Design principles we publish and follow
  • Rules first, with a language model as the fallback
  • A human in the loop for anything consequential
  • Provider-agnostic: the model is chosen on measured cost and accuracy
  • Full logging
  • Data-handling terms reviewed by counsel

Team Enablement

Training is part of every build and is also sold on its own. See Training for the five formats.

Stage 3

Sustain

Continuity Retainer

Keeps what we built running and improving: monitoring, model refresh, small enhancements on banked hours, a quarterly review, priority response, and continued coaching. Six-month minimum.

Fractional AI and Data Leadership

For clients that need direction and not only delivery: a named partner acts as part-time head of data and AI, attending leadership meetings, owning the roadmap, vetting vendors, and overseeing any in-house staff.

Governance and Assurance

Drawing on the partners' legal, audit, and controls experience: AI-use policies, review of vendor and data-processing terms, controls for regulated businesses, and audits of legal-operations cost.

Standard engagement terms

  • A master services agreement with a statement of work for each project
  • Acceptance criteria written into every statement of work as measurable success metrics
  • You own your deliverables and data; we retain our reusable tooling and templates
  • A data-handling addendum: what data we access, where it is processed, which AI providers touch it, and how long it is retained
  • A written change-order process before additional work begins

02
Our approach

Four steps, with training running alongside

Each engagement moves through the same four steps. Training does not wait for the end: it runs with the diagnostic, with every build, or on its own.

01Diagnose

What happens
We interview the owner, the operations and finance leads, and frontline staff; inventory your systems and how data moves between them; run a time study of the most repetitive workflows; and review security and data handling.
What you receive
A written report of 15 to 25 pages.
How long
Two to three weeks.
Who is involved
A partner, plus the owner's operations and finance leads.

02Plan

What happens
We rank every candidate intervention by hours saved, revenue effect, cost, and risk, and match tools to the processes we observed.
What you receive
The opportunity map, a 60-minute readout, a 90-day plan, and fixed-price quotes for the top two or three interventions.
How long
The final week of the diagnostic.
Who is involved
The partner and the owner.

03Build

What happens
We start from one of three reference architectures (a database-and-dashboard starter, a document-intake pipeline starter, or an integration-and-reporting starter), with acceptance criteria written into the statement of work.
What you receive
A working system, a runbook, and a training deck produced from the same source as the build.
How long
Fixed scope, fixed fee, agreed before work begins.
Who is involved
Partner-led delivery with your process owner.

04Sustain

What happens
Monitoring and refresh, small enhancements on banked hours, a quarterly review, and standing advisory.
What you receive
A system that keeps working, and a named advisor.
How long
A six-month minimum retainer.
Who is involved
A partner, plus your team.

Training runs alongside, not after: with the diagnostic, with every build, or on its own.

Training

Training that changes how people work

Training at this firm changes how people work; it is not an awareness session. It comes in two shapes: structured courses on AI and how to use it well, and tailored programs designed from time spent inside your business, studying how your teams actually work. Every format is live and instructor-led, on-site or virtual, built on your tools, your data, and your real tasks.

AI Foundations Workshop

Half dayWhole team, up to 20 people

Safe-use policy, prompting that works, your approved tools, hands-on practice on the team's real tasks.

Role Accelerator Workshop

Full dayOne function: finance, operations, sales, or legal

Function-specific workflows built live on your data and documents.

Team Enablement Program

Six weeks, one 90-minute session per week plus office hours6 to 15 people

Each participant automates one real task; ends with a demo day and an internal playbook.

Leadership Briefing

Two hoursOwners and executives

What is real, what is risk, how to govern AI use, what to fund next.

Public Cohort Session

One evening or a half day, monthlyMixed small-business owners

An entry-level session that leads naturally to a diagnostic.
