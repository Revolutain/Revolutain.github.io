Who we are

Three partners, one practice

A decision-science practitioner, a technology executive and educator, and an attorney and CPA.

RevolutAIn is a three-partner practice built on one observation: most owner-led businesses do not need an AI strategy. They need someone to find the two or three places where data and automation would actually pay, build them properly, and teach the team to run them. Two of us met at LSU as professor and student in the analytics program; the third has taught alongside James in the MBA program for years. We keep the firm small on purpose: the partner you meet is the partner who does the work.

The partners

Who you will work with

J. Scott Fargason

Partner · Attorney, CPA, internal audit

Scott is a Distinguished Instructor in LSU's Flores MBA Program and holds the Lee D. and Cynthia C. Bloch Distinguished Professorship in Business Law. His career sits where law, accounting, and audit meet: he advises companies on revenue enhancement and cost reduction, has audited legal departments and outside counsel for Fortune 500 companies, and wrote the Institute of Internal Auditors' practice guides on auditing legal services and intellectual property. He has been a member of the Louisiana bar since 1991. For the firm he leads governance and assurance work, engagement terms and data-handling agreements, and the cost-management discipline that puts a number on every intervention before and after it is built.

JD and BS Finance, LSU · LLM International Law, Tulane · CPA, CMA, CIA, CFE

James Davis, PhD

Partner · Technology executive and educator

Dr. James Davis has taught at LSU since 2013, where he is a Professional-in-Residence in the Stephenson Department of Entrepreneurship and Information Systems. He directed the MS in Analytics program and the Stephenson Entrepreneurship Institute, and holds a PhD in Information Systems and Decision Science from LSU. Alongside teaching, he has been a co-founder and chief technology officer, leading cloud migrations, controls audits, and technology architecture. He is a retired Army Lieutenant Colonel and two-tour combat veteran (Iraq and Afghanistan) who held command at the company and battalion levels, earned the Bronze Star, and taught decision-making to senior officers. A certified PMP and SAFe Practice Consultant, he brings the firm a formal delivery methodology, architecture and security judgment, and the classroom habits behind every training format.

PhD Information Systems and Decision Science, LSU · PMP · SAFe Practice Consultant

John Edward Schwartzenburg

Partner · Decision science and engineering

John Edward has worked as a decision scientist for the last decade. He has spent his career working within Fortune 50 corporations working with internal clients to resolve long-standing pain points. He has deployed predictive models, built dashboards, automated data environments, and delivered full-stack software solutions. In the last 5 years, his work has evolved to utilize AI for every step in the development process, allowing him to deliver products as a sole developer at the same rate as a full development team. For the firm, John Edward brings rich knowledge of cutting-edge technologies and how to use them effectively. His experience has taught him how to engage with clients, understand their pain points, and deliver tailored solutions at a rapid pace.

MBA, Tulane Freeman School · MS Analytics and BS Computer Science, LSU
