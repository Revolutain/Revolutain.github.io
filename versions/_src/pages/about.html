Who we are

Three partners, one practice

A decision-science practitioner, a technology executive and educator, and an attorney and CPA.

RevolutAIn is a three-partner practice built on one observation: most owner-led businesses do not need an AI strategy. They need someone to find the two or three places where data and automation would actually pay, build them properly, and teach the team to run them. Two of us met at LSU as professor and student in the analytics program; the third has taught alongside James in the MBA program for years. We keep the firm small on purpose: the partner you meet is the partner who does the work.

The partners

Who you will work with

J. Scott Fargason

Partner · Attorney, CPA, internal audit

Scott is a Distinguished Instructor in LSU's Flores MBA Program and holds the Lee D. and Cynthia C. Bloch Distinguished Professorship in Business Law. His career sits where law, accounting, and audit meet: he advises companies on revenue enhancement and cost reduction, has audited legal departments and outside counsel for Fortune 500 companies, and wrote the Institute of Internal Auditors' practice guides on auditing legal services and intellectual property. He has been a member of the Louisiana bar since 1991. For the firm he leads governance and assurance work, engagement terms and data-handling agreements, and the cost-management discipline that puts a number on every intervention before and after it is built.

JD and BS Finance, LSU · LLM International Law, Tulane · CPA, CMA, CIA, CFE

James Davis, PhD

Partner · Technology executive and educator

James has taught at LSU since 2013, where he is a Senior Instructor in the Stephenson Department of Entrepreneurship and Information Systems and the Flores MBA Program; he directed the MS in Analytics program and the Stephenson Entrepreneurship Institute. Alongside teaching he has been a co-founder and chief technology officer, leading cloud migrations, controls audits, and technology architecture through a merger, and he advises health and workforce startups. He is a retired Lieutenant Colonel in the US Army Reserve, where he taught decision-making to senior officers. For the firm he brings a formal delivery methodology, architecture and security judgment, and the classroom habits behind every training format.

PhD Information Systems and Decision Science, LSU · MS Computer Science · BS Mathematics · PMP, CISM, Certified Scrum Professional, SAFe Program Consultant

John Edward Schwartzenburg

Partner · Decision science and engineering

John Edward builds what the firm delivers: forecasting models, dashboards and reporting environments, data pipelines, and applications that put language models to work on real documents and records. He has spent his career doing that work inside a large enterprise and for his own clients, usually as the sole engineer, including rebuilding reporting environments after outside firms had failed, putting a production demand forecast into daily use, and shipping production applications over very large datasets in a matter of weeks. He works from written standards for security, testing, and documentation, which is what lets a small team deliver at the pace of a large one. For the firm he leads diagnostics, builds, and the hands-on labs in every training format.

MBA, Tulane Freeman School · MS Analytics and BS Computer Science, LSU
