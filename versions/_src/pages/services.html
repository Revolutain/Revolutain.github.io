Services

What we do, in the order it happens

Every engagement starts with a diagnostic. From there a client may commission a build, adopt a recommended set of tools with training for the people who will use them, or do both. Retainers, fractional leadership, and governance work keep what was built running.

Stage 1

Diagnose

Operational Diagnostic

The diagnostic opens every relationship. A partner leads it, the fee is fixed, and it takes two to three weeks.

What we do
  • Interview the owner, the operations and finance leads, and two to four frontline staff
  • Inventory the systems in use, where the data lives, how it moves, and what breaks
  • Run a time study of the most repetitive workflows, combining self-reporting with sampled observation
  • Review security and data handling: access, backups, credentials, vendor exposure
What you receive
  • A ranked opportunity map: each intervention with estimated hours saved, revenue effect, cost, and risk
  • A recommended set of tools matched to the processes we observed, and a plan for training the team to use them
  • A written report, a 60-minute readout with the owner, and a 90-day plan
  • Fixed-price quotes for the top two or three interventions
How long

Two to three weeks.

Who is involved

A partner, with the owner and the operations and finance leads.

How it is priced

Fixed fee, credited against any build started within 60 days.

Stage 2

Build and Train

Decision Science Builds

Models and dashboards that turn the data you already have into decisions.

  • Demand forecasts
  • Pricing and margin analysis
  • KPI frameworks with an executive dashboard
  • Customer or job profitability
  • Capacity and scheduling
  • Forecast-driven budgets

Infrastructure and Data Platform Builds

The plumbing: one governed place for your data and reliable connections between the systems you run.

  • Spreadsheets consolidated into a governed database
  • Point-of-sale, CRM, accounting, and scheduling systems integrated
  • A reporting layer over the systems you already run
  • Migration off legacy Access and Excel
  • Backups, access control, credential hygiene, and cloud right-sizing

Workflow Automation and LLM Integration

Automation for the repetitive work, with language models where rules are not enough.

  • Document intake and extraction into structured records
  • Voice-to-record documentation
  • Drafting assistants grounded in your own documents
  • Email and ticket triage
  • Pipelines that watch your data and escalate exceptions

How we build

The same rules apply to every build, and we put them in writing.

  • Rules first, with a language model as the fallback
  • A human in the loop for anything consequential
  • Provider-agnostic: the model is chosen on measured cost and accuracy
  • Every automated decision is logged
  • Data-handling terms reviewed by counsel

Training runs alongside, not after: with the diagnostic, with every build, or on its own.

Stage 3

Sustain

Continuity Retainer

Keeps what we built running and improving: monitoring, model refresh, small enhancements on banked hours, a quarterly review, priority response, and continued coaching.

Fractional AI and Data Leadership

For clients that need direction and not only delivery: a named partner acts as part-time head of data and AI, attending leadership meetings, owning the roadmap, vetting vendors, and overseeing any in-house staff.

Governance and Assurance

Drawing on the partners' legal, audit, and controls experience: AI-use policies, review of vendor and data-processing terms, controls for regulated businesses, and audits of legal-operations cost.

How long

Six-month minimum.

Who is involved

A named partner, plus your team.

How it is priced

Monthly retainer.

Terms

Standard engagement terms

  • A master services agreement with a statement of work for each project
  • Acceptance criteria written into every statement of work as measurable success metrics
  • You own your deliverables and data; we retain our reusable tooling and templates
  • A data-handling addendum: what data we access, where it is processed, which AI providers touch it, and how long it is retained
  • A written change-order process before additional work begins
