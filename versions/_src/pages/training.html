Training

Training that changes how people work

Our training is built on your tools, your data, and tasks your team already does. It comes in two kinds: structured courses on using AI well, and programs tailored to your business after we have watched how your teams work. Every format is live and instructor-led, on-site or virtual.

Formats

Five formats

AI Foundations Workshop

Half dayWhole team, up to 20 people

A safe-use policy, prompting that works, your approved tools, and hands-on practice on the team's real tasks.

Role Accelerator Workshop

Full dayOne function: finance, operations, sales, or legal

Function-specific workflows built live on your data and documents.

Team Enablement Program

Six weeks, one 90-minute session a week plus office hours6 to 15 people

Each participant automates one real task. The program ends with a demo day and an internal playbook.

Leadership Briefing

Two hoursOwners and executives

What is real, what is risk, how to govern AI use, and what to fund next.

Public Cohort Session

One evening or a half day, monthlySmall-business owners

An open, entry-level session for owners who want to see what this looks like before committing to anything.
