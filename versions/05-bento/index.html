How engagements run

Every engagement follows three stages

Stage 1

Diagnose

A two-to-three-week Operational Diagnostic, led by a partner, that ends in a ranked opportunity map and fixed-price quotes.

Stage 2

Build and Train

Fixed-scope builds on your data and systems, and training for the people who will use them, together or on their own.

Stage 3

Sustain

A retainer, a fractional data and AI lead, or a governance review that keeps what was built working and keeps us at the table.

Our approach

Four steps, with training running alongside

01

Diagnose

Interviews, a systems and data inventory, a time study, and a security review.

02

Plan

Interventions ranked by hours saved, revenue effect, cost, and risk; a 90-day plan.

03

Build

From one of three reference architectures, with acceptance criteria in the statement of work.

04

Sustain

Monitoring, refresh, banked hours, a quarterly review, and a named advisor.

Training runs alongside, not after: with the diagnostic, with every build, or on its own.
