Training

Training that changes how people work

Training at this firm changes how people work; it is not an awareness session. It comes in two shapes: structured courses on AI and how to use it well, and tailored programs designed from time spent inside your business, studying how your teams actually work. Every format is live and instructor-led, on-site or virtual, built on your tools, your data, and your real tasks.

Formats

Five formats

T-01

AI Foundations Workshop

Half dayWhole team, up to 20 people

Safe-use policy, prompting that works, your approved tools, hands-on practice on the team's real tasks.

T-02

Role Accelerator Workshop

Full dayOne function: finance, operations, sales, or legal

Function-specific workflows built live on your data and documents.

T-03

Team Enablement Program

Six weeks, one 90-minute session per week plus office hours6 to 15 people

Each participant automates one real task; ends with a demo day and an internal playbook.

T-04

Leadership Briefing

Two hoursOwners and executives

What is real, what is risk, how to govern AI use, what to fund next.

T-05

Public Cohort Session

One evening or a half day, monthlyMixed small-business owners

An entry-level session that leads naturally to a diagnostic.
