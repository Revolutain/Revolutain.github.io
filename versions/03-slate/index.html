Our approach

Four steps, with training running alongside

01

Diagnose

Interviews, a systems and data inventory, a time study, and a security review. Two to three weeks.

02

Plan

Interventions ranked by hours saved, revenue effect, cost, and risk; a 90-day plan; fixed-price quotes.

03

Build

From one of three reference architectures, with acceptance criteria written into the statement of work.

04

Sustain

Monitoring, refresh, banked hours, a quarterly review, and a named advisor.

+

Training runs alongside, not after

With the diagnostic, with every build, or on its own.
